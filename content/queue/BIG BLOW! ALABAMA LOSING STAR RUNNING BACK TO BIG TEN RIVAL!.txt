First, let’s get to know the player, because a lot of Alabama fans might not know much about Nigel Newkirk yet.

Newkirk is a 2027 running back out of Gainesville High School in Georgia. He’s listed at five-foot-eleven and about one hundred eighty-five pounds, and he’s the kind of back who runs physically downhill but also has the explosive speed to break off long runs. Look at what he did as a junior. One thousand four hundred ninety-six rushing yards. Nineteen touchdowns. Eight point one yards per carry. Eight yards every single time he touched the ball! That’s production. That’s a guy who can change a game.

And the rankings back it up. The 247Sports Composite has him as the two hundred twenty-sixth player in the country, the sixteenth-best running back in the 2027 class, and the twenty-fifth-best prospect in the state of Georgia. And at the time of his commitment, Rivals had him even higher, as the number six running back in the Rivals300. When he committed, he was the third-highest-ranked prospect in Alabama’s 2027 class. So this isn’t some random name. This is one of the cornerstone pieces of the class.

Now, here’s the part of the story I really want you to hear, because it tells you why Alabama won this recruitment in the first place. Newkirk committed to the Crimson Tide on May thirty-first, right after his official visit to Tuscaloosa. He had originally planned to make his decision around July first. But the visit changed everything. In his words, he “just saw everything that I wanted to see.” That’s how powerful that weekend was.

And the biggest reason? Relationships. Newkirk built a really strong bond with Alabama running backs coach Robert Gillespie. He described Gillespie as being like “a second father” to him. That’s not something a kid says lightly. He also talked about DeBoer, saying the head coach is cheerful off the field but intense on it. And he said Alabama has “always made me feel wanted, and my visits there were always great.” He visited Alabama more than any other school. And some of his former high school teammates are already in Tuscaloosa, including freshman Xavier Griffin, who was one of the top linebackers in the country, and Jamarion Matthews. Griffin was rated as the number one linebacker in the 2026 class when he committed to Alabama, so Newkirk has watched one of his teammates go through this exact process and end up in Tuscaloosa. Having friends already on campus who can tell you what life is really like inside the program is a huge advantage in a recruiting battle.

So when you hear that Newkirk is taking a visit somewhere else, you need to remember all of that context. This is a kid who has deep roots with this program. He chose Alabama over Ohio State, LSU and Michigan. He didn’t pick the Tide by accident.

And let’s talk a little more about Robert Gillespie, because he might be the single most important person in this entire recruitment. Gillespie has been Alabama’s running backs coach since 2021. He came to Tuscaloosa under Nick Saban, and when Kalen DeBoer took over, he kept Gillespie on staff. That tells you how highly he’s regarded. Before Alabama, Gillespie spent years coaching running backs at Tennessee, where he worked with Alvin Kamara. At Alabama, he coached Jahmyr Gibbs, who went twelfth overall in the NFL Draft. So when Gillespie sits down in a recruit’s living room and talks about developing running backs for the next level, he has the résumé to back it up. That’s a big part of why Newkirk called him “a second father.” And that’s exactly the kind of bond that can hold a commitment together when another program comes calling.

So the Buckeyes have a need, and they’re going back to a player they already knew well. And Ohio State is going to have a strong pitch. A game-day atmosphere like that is designed to impress a recruit. Alabama fans should respect that. But Alabama has a pitch too, and it’s a pretty good one: six national championships under Nick Saban, a long list of running backs who became NFL players, and a coaching staff that has already earned this young man’s trust.

Now, I want to be fair and balanced about this. An official visit from a committed player is a big deal. There’s no use sugarcoating it. When a commitment takes an official visit to a school like Ohio State, especially for a game like Ohio State against Oregon, that’s a serious recruiting moment. Some of the Alabama sites are already openly talking about the possibility of a flip, and the staff clearly takes it seriously.

But here’s the other side. Commits take visits all the time in modern college football. Players want to see every option before they sign, and that’s their right. A visit is not a decommitment. Plenty of committed recruits take official visits elsewhere and still end up signing with the school they originally picked. Sometimes a visit actually makes a commitment stronger, because the player sees the other option up close and realizes the place he already chose is still the right fit for him. And sometimes, of course, a visit leads to a flip. That’s recruiting. Nobody can promise you which way this goes, and anybody who says they know for sure is guessing. Newkirk hasn’t said anything negative about Alabama. And remember, the relationship with Robert Gillespie is the foundation of this whole recruitment. In November 2025, Kirkpatrick was arrested after a traffic stop on the Strip in Tuscaloosa and charged with reckless endangerment, attempting to elude and speeding.
